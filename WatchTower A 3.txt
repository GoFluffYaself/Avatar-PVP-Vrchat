Cyberdark Armed Forces is a avatar PvP group loosely based on Abyssal Units from 
Kantai Collection and the Cybran Nation from Supreme Commander, though it takes inspiration from many other things as well. 

CDAF is a division of the faction known as the Cyberdark Collective. 
Hailing from a strange and hostile dimension called the Abyss (aka Hungering Abyss), the Collective is a society that values knowledge above almost all else, 
and is adept at technology, magic and the fusion of the two.
