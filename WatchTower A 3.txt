The Militants of Ethrafa
**What does it mean to be human?**
Is it the willingness to throw ourselves into danger for the sake of our loved ones, or is it our capacity to dominate others, to bare fear in ourselves and those around us?
We are **The Militants of Ethrafa** and in our universe of “Eon” we are the embodiment of the belief that it is the ambition to create, the capacity of autonomy, and the will to manifest one’s own goals that makes one human. 
Not allowing even our own flesh to prevent us from exercising our ability to mold our realities into existence.
- Become a creature once human now evolved
- Pick your bloodline and genetic enhancements, apply them in PvP combat
- Fight in **Multi-month** wars against our tyrannical enemies
- Build bonds through blood
- Climb the pyramid of power
- Become a legend!
